## 2. Team Goals and Expectations
- **Project Goal:** Apply Computational Thinking concepts (Decomposition, Pattern Recognition, Abstraction, Algorithms) to design and build a functional Smart Virtual Assistant. The assistant should process user inputs, apply deterministic logic and AI routing, and produce verified, reliable outputs.
- **Target Grade/Quality:** We aim for an **A grade (9.0+)**. System logic must be modular, deterministic algorithms well-documented (flowcharts/pseudocode), and computational decisions backed by empirical evidence and tests.
- **Effort Commitment:** Each member commits to spending **5–7 hours per week** on this project (meetings, architecture drafting, coding, testing, and report writing).

## 5. Meetings
- **Frequency & Time:** Twice weekly.
  - Wednesday at 8:00 PM (Online via Discord).
  - Saturday at 9:00 AM (In-person at Campus Library/Study Room).
- **Meeting Rules:**
  - **Agenda:** Posted by the Leader at least 12 hours before each meeting.
  - **Minutes:** Meeting notes rotated sequentially among members in shared Google Docs.
  - **Punctuality:** Maximum allowable delay is 10 minutes.
  - **Absence:** Notify the group at least **24 hours in advance** with legitimate justification.

---

## 6. Work Process and Standards
- **Task Tracking:** Maintained via GitHub Projects / Trello (Backlog, In Progress, Review, Done).
- **CT Standards:** Every feature must undergo formal **Decomposition** and **Pseudocode/Flowchart design** prior to implementation.
- **Code Standards:** 
  - Meaningful naming conventions and modular functional units.
  - Inline comments explaining architectural intent, assumptions, and edge cases.
  - Git branching: `feature/<feature-name>`, `fix/<bug-name>`, merged only via reviewed Pull Requests (PRs).
- **Internal Deadlines:** Deliver milestones **48 hours** prior to official deadlines to guarantee integration and regression testing.

---

## 7. Decision Making
- **Mechanism:** Democratic majority vote (minimum 3 out of 5 affirmative votes).
- **Tie-Breaker:** In deadlocks, the **Team Leader** holds the deciding vote to ensure progress.

---

## 8. Conflict Resolution
1. **Direct Negotiation:** Concerned parties discuss constructively in private.
2. **Team Mediation:** If unresolved within 24 hours, the issue is escalated to the full team for structured discussion.
3. **Faculty Consultation:** If an impasse persists, the Leader formally consults course instructors or TAs for arbitration.

---

## 9. Accountability and Consequences
- **Breaches:** Unexcused absence, missed deadlines, substandard deliverables, or persistent free-riding.
- **Escalation Path:**
  - *1st Offense:* Constructive peer feedback and support check-in.
  - *2nd Offense:* Formal written notice logged in group records.
  - *3rd Offense:* Formal report to course instructors; member peer-evaluation weight adjusted to 0% for the milestone.

---

## 10. Academic Integrity and Responsible AI Use
- **Plagiarism:** Zero tolerance for copied or unreferenced third-party code and text.
- **AI Tool Boundaries:** Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude) may assist in syntax verification, boilerplate generation, and brainstorming. AI must not generate the core computational logic unverified.
- **Accountability:** Every team member must be able to justify, defend, and explain line-by-line any logic submitted under their name.

---

## 11. Amendments
- Contract modifications require discussion during an official team meeting and agreement from **at least 3 out of 5 members**.
